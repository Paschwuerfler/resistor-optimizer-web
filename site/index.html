This is an unofficial browser re-implementation of Resistor Optimizer, originally written by Janne Ahonen as a Win32/MFC application. All credit for the idea, the design of the tool and the bundled resistor series (.ser) files goes to him — see the original project page for the Windows version. Bugs in this port are the port's own, not the original's.

Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
